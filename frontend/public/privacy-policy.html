Privacy Policy — Cozy Blissful Salon & Spa

Effective date: October 2, 2026 • Last updated: October 2, 2026

Cozy Blissful Salon & Spa (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates a luxury spa & salon appointment booking platform. This policy explains what we collect when you use our website and sign in — including with Facebook Login (Meta) — how we use it, and your choices. Contact: cozyblissfulspa@gmail.com • Facebook: facebook.com/cozyblissful.

1. Facebook Login — data we receive and why

When you tap “Continue with Facebook” on our Login/Register page, Meta shares limited data via the Facebook SDK / OAuth dialog. We request only:

  • public_profile — name, first name, last name, Facebook user ID (account identification & personalization).
  • email — Facebook email address (account identifier, login matching, booking confirmations, recovery).

Server-side we verify each access token against the Meta Graph API (debug_token + /me?fields=id,name,first_name,last_name,email) with our App ID / Secret before fetching any profile. We do not request friends, photos, birthday, gender, likes, or posts. If no email is shared, we ask you to enter one on the Register page.

2. How we use your information

  • Authentication: match Facebook email to an existing account or pre-fill registration (name/email) to finish signup with a password.
  • Bookings: create/manage appointments (pending → confirmed → in-progress → completed), therapist assignment, rescheduling, cancellations, reminders.
  • Communications: booking confirmation, approval, reminder, and welcome emails.
  • Security: rate-limit logins, audit logs, fraud prevention.
  • Payments: PayMongo checkout metadata (we never store full card numbers).

We never sell personal data and never use Facebook data for third-party advertising.

3. Other data we collect

  • Account data you provide: name, email, bcrypt-hashed password, phone (optional), preferences, appointment history.
  • Google Sign-In (optional): verified ID token → name/email, same use as above.
  • Automatic: IP, device/browser, login timestamps, audit logs.
  • Cookies/local storage: 7-day Sanctum session token, remembered email (“Remember me”), theme/cart. No third-party ad trackers.

4. Sharing

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — Facebook Login SDK / Graph API verification.
  • Google LLC — only if you use Google sign-in.
  • PayMongo — payment processing.
  • Email provider (SMTP/Gmail) — transactional emails.
  • Hosting provider — secure app/database hosting.

5. Retention

Account/booking records kept while active and as required for business/tax/audit (typically up to 5 years for transaction logs). Session tokens expire after 7 days. Earlier deletion available on request.

6. Security

Bcrypt (12 rounds), server-side social-token verification, HTTPS, role-based access (admin/staff/therapist/client), Sanctum Bearer tokens, login throttles, transactions + audit logs.

7. Your rights — access, correction & deletion

  • Access/correct via your dashboard or by email.
  • Delete: email cozyblissfulspa@gmail.com with subject “Data deletion request” from your account email (include full name + account email). We verify ownership and delete account, personal booking identifiers, and Facebook-derived fields within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Disconnect Facebook: Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites → remove “Cozy Blissful”. This stops future sharing; stored data deleted per above.

8. Children

Not directed to children under 13. Contact us to delete any inadvertently collected child data.

9. Changes

Updates posted here with a new “Last updated” date; material changes notified by email/in-app where appropriate.
